Changelog

Changes to the shop itself, newest first. Product news lives on the store pages.

2026-09-18 — Forgotten passwords

  • Resetting a forgotten password now looks like the rest of the shop, from the request form through to the confirmation, instead of dropping you on a bare default page.
  • If you sign in with TG11 instead, the reset page says so and points you to the right place, rather than quietly failing to find an account.

2026-09-14 — Information pages

  • About, Terms of Sale, Privacy, Guidelines, FAQ, Support, Status and this changelog, linked from every footer.
  • The terms now say plainly how made-to-order goods work: what we replace without argument, and where a change of mind stands.

2026-09-13 — More ways to pay

  • PayPal at checkout, settled on PayPal's own signed confirmation rather than on the browser coming back.
  • Fox Pay alongside Stripe, which also opens the door to a crypto invoice.
  • One shared completion path behind all three, so an order is marked paid once and only once, whichever route took the money.
  • The payment choice appears whenever there is actually a choice to make.

2026-09-13 — Sign in with TG11

  • TG11 single sign-on: one identity across the TG11 services, with two-factor authentication if you turn it on there. Local shop accounts still work.
  • Free-shipping threshold wired through checkout properly, and a shipping option that was being quoted but could not be fulfilled was removed rather than left to disappoint people.

Earlier in 2026

  • Guest checkout, so an order does not require an account.
  • Tax handling corrections.
  • Product media uploads, page hero banners, and link previews for products and store pages.
  • Marketplace connectors for listings and orders, and the fulfilment pipeline behind them.

Maintained by hand from the release history, so it records what changed for you rather than every commit.
